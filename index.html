Hi, I'm Dirk.

Aviation journalist at ch-aviation, covering airlines across the Asia-Pacific from Davao, Philippines.

About

I've been fascinated by things that fly for as long as I can remember, and I started sharing that passion through aviation media in 2013, long before I thought it would become my profession.

Much of my work means digging through fleet and schedule data, filings, regulatory documents and announcements to understand not just what airlines are doing, but why. I'm especially interested in how airlines actually work: aircraft deployment, networks, and the operational decisions and constraints behind them.

I also have a background in computer engineering, software development, databases and humanitarian work. It's an unusual mix, but it shapes how I approach aviation: through data, systems, people and communication.
