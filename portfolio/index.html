Red Cross Youth

EventHub

Event sign-ups for the Red Cross Youth in Davao City. People register online, organizers approve them against a seat limit, and everyone who gets in receives a QR ticket for check-in on the day.

Test event with made-up registrations. Click any screenshot to see it in full.

Emergency response

PRC Volunteer Mobilization System

My college thesis, built for the Philippine Red Cross Davao City Chapter. Staff track incidents by alert level, check who's available, and deploy volunteers. The first version sent text alerts through a GSM modem.

Game in development

Aviocracy

An airline management game for Mac and Windows. You start an airline with real aircraft types at real airports, plan every route and schedule yourself, and compete with AI airlines. The clock runs in real time and doesn't stop when you close the game, so there's always something waiting when you come back.

Development build with a test airline.

News publishing

CTRL+PH

A Philippine tech news site. I built the WordPress theme, plus Desk, a separate app where story leads are logged and followed up until they're published.

Local copies with placeholder stories and leads.

Personal finance

Paldo

A personal finance tracker. It keeps bank accounts, credit cards, loans and monthly bills in one place, and tells me how much cash I'll have left once everything due before a given date is paid.

Local demo with made-up numbers.

More projects

  • VATSIM Flight Companion

    A vPilot plugin that sends my VATSIM messages and flight milestones to Telegram, so I can step away from the sim PC on a long flight and still reply.

  • Freelance tax calculator

    Works out quarterly and annual BIR income tax for Philippine freelancers, including the 8% flat-rate option.

  • Event project tracker

    Tasks, deadlines and approvals for event committees, with one overview for whoever is running the whole event.

  • Pictura

    Paste in a web page address and it pulls out every image on the page, so you can look through them and download the ones you want.
